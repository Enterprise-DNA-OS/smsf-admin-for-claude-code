---
description: Everything that wants a decision today, worst first: compliance breaches, pensions behind, members near a cap, jobs stuck or late, audit queries waiting, bank lines to code.
---

1. Run `npm run smsf -- attention`.
2. Read it in order:
   - **Compliance** first. Each line is a rule the records say has been broken. Say which fund and which member, and what the auditor will see.
   - **Pensions.** Nothing paid yet, or behind the pace the minimum needs by 30 June.
   - **Contributions.** Members near a cap, members turning 75.
   - **Jobs and audit.** Records not in, jobs stuck in a stage, queries waiting.
   - **Bank.** Lines nobody has coded: until they are coded, the caps and the pension figures are incomplete.
3. For each item give the one action and who owns it (the manager on the fund, the trustee, the auditor).
4. Anything that needs a letter or email is drafted to `drafts/`, never sent.

If the operator asks "what should I do today", pick the top three and say why those three.
