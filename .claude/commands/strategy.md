---
description: Record that the trustees reviewed the fund's investment strategy, and whether the review considered insurance for the members.
---

1. Ask for the date of the trustees' minute and whether it records that insurance was considered.
2. `npm run smsf -- strategy <fund> --date=YYYY-MM-DD [--insurance]`. Leave out `--insurance` if the minute does not say it was considered.
3. Run `npm run smsf -- compliance` and confirm R9 is clear for the fund.
