---
description: Record a money movement that is not a contribution or a pension: income, an expense, a purchase, a sale, a rollover, tax.
---

1. `npm run smsf -- add <fund> <income|expense|purchase|sale|rollover-in|rollover-out|lump-sum|tax> <amount> [--date] [--member] [--holding] [--desc]`.
2. Contributions go through `/contributions` and pensions through `/pensions`, so the caps and minimums are checked.
