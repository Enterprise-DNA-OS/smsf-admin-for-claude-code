description: The job board for last year's accounts and audit: every fund by stage, days in the stage, who has it, the lodgment date and open queries.
---

1. Run `npm run smsf -- jobs` (a year: `--fy=2025-26`; a stage: `--stage=review`; a person: `--who=AL`).
2. Present the board. Lead with returns past their due date, then jobs inside 45 days of it that are not with the auditor yet, then jobs stuck more than three weeks.
