---
description: Draft the note to a member over or close to a contribution cap: the figures, the cap, what is left, and that their adviser should look at it before the next contribution.
---

1. Run `npm run smsf -- member <name> --json`.
2. Write `drafts/cap-warning-<member>.md`: contributions so far this year by type, the cap (with carry-forward only if their balance allows it), the room left or the excess, and the date of the next contribution if one is regular (salary sacrifice, SG).
3. No advice on what to do about an excess: say the options exist and that their adviser or the practice will talk them through it.
4. Never send it.
