---
description: The June checklist before 30 June: pension minimums still to pay, members over a cap, carry-forward room still usable, notices of intent needed, strategies to review.
---

1. Run `npm run smsf -- eofy`.
2. Present the five lists in order. For each, the action and who owns it.
3. Offer the letters: `/draft-pension-reminder` for every pension with something left to pay, `/draft-cap-warning` for anyone over or near a cap.
4. Notices of intent: a member claiming a deduction for a personal contribution gives the fund a notice of intent and gets the fund's acknowledgement before they lodge their own return. List who needs one.
